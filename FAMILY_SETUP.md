# Getting set up with Family Recipes

1. On your phone, tap the invite link I sent you.
2. Tap **Create account** (or **Sign in** if you already have one). Use the same email address the invite was sent to.
3. Tap **Accept invite**. You'll now see our shared grocery lists, pantry, recipes, and meal plan.
4. Add the app to your home screen so it opens like a regular app:
   - **iPhone:** open it in Safari, tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
   - **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
5. From now on, open it from the home-screen icon. It even works offline — anything you check off or add will sync when you're back online.

If the link says it's expired or already used, just ask me for a fresh one — invite links are single-use.
